# Original Results Summary

This file summarizes the supplied statistical report at a high level. It is not a raw-data
release.

## Study 1

- Liver selenium differed by treatment group, changed over time, and had a significant
  treatment-by-time interaction.
- Whole-blood selenium differed by treatment group, changed over time, and had a significant
  treatment-by-time interaction.
- Repeat-breeding frequency differed among treatment groups in the global test.
- Final pregnancy outcome differed among treatment groups in the global test.
- Baseline body weight did not show evidence of group imbalance and did not explain final
  pregnancy outcome.
- Reproductive-window whole-blood selenium analyses were treated as supporting
  associations, not independent causal proof.

## Study 2

- Whole-blood selenium differed by treatment group, changed over time, and had a significant
  treatment-by-time interaction.
- Actual breeding-cycle distribution did not meet the 0.05 threshold in the global primary
  test.
- Ultrasound reproductive-efficiency outcomes differed by treatment group, but actual
  breeding-cycle records were preferred for primary cycle-efficiency interpretation.
- Baseline body weight did not show evidence of group imbalance and did not explain final
  pregnancy outcome.
- Reproductive-window and full-follow-up selenium models were interpreted as supporting or
  secondary associations.

## Interpretation Notes Carried Into The Public Project

- Significant treatment-by-time interactions mean trajectories should be emphasized rather
  than only averaged treatment main effects.
- Outcome-stratified selenium models are supporting associations.
- Full-follow-up selenium models include measurements after breeding and should not be used
  to claim later selenium values caused earlier outcomes.
- Control-vs-treatment contrasts are secondary to global tests.
- Small samples require cautious language.
- If each treatment group is in one pen, pen and treatment are confounded.
